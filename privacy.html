Privacy policy

Last updated 26 October 2026.

The short version

getIRL collects no data. Everything it knows stays on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no servers.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your profiles: their names, schedules and settings, and the apps, categories and websites you chose to block.
  • Your history: when blocks started and ended, unlocks, and the hours protected.
  • The name you gave the app, if any, used only to greet you.

This is stored in the app's own storage on the device and is removed when you delete the app.

Screen Time

getIRL uses Apple's Screen Time frameworks (Family Controls, Managed Settings and Device Activity) to block the apps you choose. Apple gives the app opaque tokens for your choices: getIRL never learns which apps or websites they are, never sees your app usage, and sends nothing anywhere.

Notifications

Notifications (the unlock hand-off and the morning recap) are created on the device. Nothing is sent through a server.

Sharing

getIRL shares nothing with anyone, because it collects nothing. There are no third-party SDKs.

Children

getIRL is a self-control tool for the person using the iPhone. It is not designed for parental control and collects no information from anyone.
